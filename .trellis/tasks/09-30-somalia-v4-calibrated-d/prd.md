## Goal

Establish the best attainable out-of-sample performance of calibrated D separately for observed-assessment and validity-expanded Somalia scenarios. The result should explain performance across evaluation years and forecast horizons under each scenario's own label population, with traceable predictions and honest coverage. Best attainable means within the declared procedure and search budget, not a mathematical performance bound. V3 supplies reusable definitions and evidence; preserving its training settings or comparing against its scores is not an objective.
