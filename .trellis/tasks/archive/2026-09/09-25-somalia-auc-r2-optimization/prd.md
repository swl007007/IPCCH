# Somalia Phase 3+ population-share optimization

## Goal

Improve Somalia Phase 3+ population-percentage prediction using the existing XGBoost experiment. Optimize nowcasting with D as the main specification and A-C as baselines. Prioritize q3 prediction error strictly, use crisis AUC only to break numerical ties, and report binary F1/recall as secondary diagnostics. Retrain and report 3m, 6m and 12m without horizon-specific optimization. The accepted decisions and their boundaries are recorded below.

Version: 0.2, 2026-09-25. Status: G1-G5 resolved; final technical review accepted 2026-09-25. Implementation and training authorized per implement.md. Brainstorm -> written spec -> grill has been followed. Concrete technical defaults in `design.md` await final acceptance, not another open-ended interview.

## Requirements

- R1. Make Phase 3+ population share primary, crisis ROC AUC secondary, then F1/recall. Replace primary F2 selection. Do not substitute average R2 across other cumulative targets for q3 R2.
- R2. Focus optimization on nowcasting (H=0). Retrain H=3/6/12 and report their supported results, without horizon-specific hyperparameter, formulation or calibration-method searches and without using their scores to steer optimization. They are not a focus of the report. Preserve explicit unavailable statuses where source/support gaps prevent a fit; retraining does not waive missing oracle evidence. Recipe transfer and permitted calibration refitting are defined in R10 and design section5.
- R3. Align candidate selection and calibration with share prediction; justify new targets/losses/models against reusable existing alternatives.
- R4. Preserve model/calibration-fitting origin cutoffs and target self-exclusion. G2 approved 2026-09-25: nowcasting candidate/model/calibration/threshold selection uses training-period temporal validation only; that fold's 2025 or 2026 evaluation outcomes must not select its recipe. The 2026 fold may still use 2025 labels inside its approved 2023-2025 training window. Disclose prior evaluation-year feedback; G1's long-horizon recipe-transfer exception does not expand H0 selection labels.
- R5. Compare methods and share persistence on identical keys within year/horizon. Preserve v1 artifacts. Report raw and calibrated q3 R2 separately, prediction bias/error and unavailable support; averages must not conceal poor q3 performance.
- R6. Resolve requirements, write the spec, then grill before implementation planning/authorization.
- R7. Approved 2026-09-25: add a persistence-residual candidate alongside direct q3 regression. For each row, let b be the latest valid historical q3 available under U<=O and U<T. Predict delta=q3-b and reconstruct q3_hat=b+delta_hat. Construct b separately at each training/validation/test row's own origin. Retain squared-error regression as the existing starting loss; compare errors on reconstructed population shares, not residual-space R2. This approval adds a candidate, not an asserted improvement or authorization to train.
- R8. Missing-history rule approved 2026-09-25: when no eligible historical q3 baseline exists, use the corresponding direct q3 model, retain the sample and record an explicit fallback flag. Report both full-cohort results of this hybrid rule and matched comparisons restricted to history-supported rows. Do not fabricate a historical baseline or claim residual predictions for fallback rows. Apply the same prediction rule during validation and evaluation; a missing historical baseline does not define a residual training target.
- R9. For nowcasting optimization, select by q3 RMSE on held-out temporal validation predictions from the complete prediction procedure, including optional calibration. On identical evaluation keys and weights, minimizing RMSE is equivalent to maximizing q3 R2; score reconstructed shares for residual models. Fit calibration only with earlier eligible data, then assess it on subsequent validation months not used to fit that mapping. Preserve original uncalibrated R2 and signed error alongside final-pipeline metrics. Detailed chronological support rules are proposed in design section4; the approved calibration inventory is R13 and strict metric priority is R15. Per R2, do not run this candidate search separately for H=3/6/12.
- R10. Approved 2026-09-25: for H=3/6/12, keep the model configuration and calibration method fixed but refit model parameters and, when calibration is enabled, its mapping using that horizon's origin-eligible historical data/predictions. Refitting is allowed; horizon-specific configuration/method selection is not. Calibration input predictions retain temporal out-of-sample provenance; receiving-origin future labels remain excluded from base-model and mapping fitting. G1 explicitly supersedes the earlier recipe-selection cutoff: these secondary horizons may inherit the corresponding nowcasting recipe even when its selection labels postdate the receiving origin. Record recipe source/cutoff, the resulting selection leakage, and calibration-fitting keys separately; do not describe these ancillary results as fully origin-valid forecasts. An identity recipe has no fitted mapping.
- R11. Model scope approved 2026-09-25: retain the existing cumulative-regression ensemble structure and use q3 predictions as the secondary crisis-ranking score; do not add an independent crisis classifier this round. Report ROC AUC for raw and final (potentially calibrated) q3 scores separately against reported phase>=3. Predicted population share is not a calibrated probability that an area is in crisis. Calibration can introduce ties or change cross-origin rankings, so do not assume its AUC equals raw-score AUC.
- R12. Arm roles approved 2026-09-25: D is the main specification; A-C are comparison baselines. Retain A-D direct-share comparisons and add the residual candidate only within D. Do not add historical q3 to A-C implicitly. Keep D-direct and D-residual results visible, and select the reported preferred D procedure using eligible temporal validation rather than its outer-test score. The detailed search budget and preferred-procedure selection contract are proposed in `design.md`.
- R13. G3 approved 2026-09-25: restrict q3 calibration candidates to no learned correction, mean-bias shift, and isotonic, evaluated by held-out chronological q3 RMSE. Final shares are bounded to [0,1] after direct prediction or residual reconstruction and any learned mapping; preserve unbounded raw predictions/metrics and clipping counts. No calibration-family expansion based on outer-test outcomes. This bounds population shares, not residual changes, and no-calibration means no learned correction.
- R14. G4 approved 2026-09-25: secondary binary crisis F1/recall/precision uses final q3>=0.2 against reported overall_phase>=3, without rounding or threshold tuning. Preserve the existing ordinal phase reconstruction and its metrics as separately named diagnostics, with disagreement counts. This threshold does not redefine reported truth. A gain in share R2 may coexist with lower binary F1/recall and must be reported honestly.
- R15. G5 approved 2026-09-25: select strictly by minimum held-out final-q3 RMSE; higher AUC does not justify substantively worse RMSE. Use AUC only for numerical RMSE ties. No AUC floor, weighted composite, F1/recall optimization or practical RMSE-sacrifice tolerance is introduced. The numerical tolerance and remaining deterministic tie order are specified in design section4.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Final spec defines share units, target, loss, selection score, aggregation, secondary AUC truth/score, calibration validation and threshold policy.
- Included horizons, information sets, cohort rules and evaluation interpretation are explicit.
- Run inventory distinguishes H0 optimization from H3/H6/H12 fixed-recipe retraining/reporting, records the source of frozen choices, and preserves unavailable cells without expanding the search.
- H3/H6/H12 model and calibration refits preserve the fixed configuration/method, with eligible source dates and calibration prediction provenance available for replay.
- Comparisons against v1 and share persistence are replayable on matched keys; no guaranteed performance gain.
- The direct and residual candidates are compared on original q3 units with identical scoring keys; residual baselines have replayable source dates and obey each row's cutoff.
- Missing-history rows retain predictions through the declared direct-model fallback, with counts/flags and separately replayable history-supported comparisons.
- Candidate selection is replayable from held-out final-pipeline q3 predictions; calibration-fitting and calibration-scoring keys/dates establish the chronological separation, and a no-calibration candidate is included.
- Selected candidates minimize final-q3 RMSE; AUC affects only numerical ties, with no threshold tuning or outer-score selection.
- Calibration inventory contains exactly the three G3 methods; final shares obey [0,1], while raw predictions and the effect of bounding remain auditable.
- Raw/final crisis ROC AUC uses declared q3 score columns and the reported-phase binary truth, without an independent classifier or probability mislabeling.
- Binary F1/recall uses the fixed final-q3 .2 threshold; legacy ordinal metrics remain separately identified and no threshold is selected on validation or test outcomes in this round.
- A-C retain their information sets; the residual-vs-direct contrast occurs within D. Baseline comparisons identify whether D also received target-formulation selection, rather than attributing all gain to rich-history features.
- User-owned decisions are recorded before final spec review and subsequent grill.

## Out of scope and interpretation

No independent crisis classifier, new raw exposure acquisition, source-data overwrites, residual targets for A-C, or horizon-specific optimization at H3/H6/H12. Preserve the prior Somalia scope, two candidate-year windows, normalized targets, feature availability rules and within-year/horizon comparisons unless a subsequent grill decision explicitly changes them. No new untouched-test claim for already inspected 2025/2026 outcomes. No guaranteed performance gain or claim that biased q3 predictions prove an erroneous R2 calculation.
